# B/X RPG

A new, self-contained, text-first browser RPG foundation derived architecturally from the Known World project, but governed exclusively by the original 1981 Moldvay Basic and Cook/Marsh Expert rules. Its AI referee proposes actions; a deterministic engine owns mechanics and state.

The repository is being initialized at checkpoint v0.1.0. See `AGENTS.md`, `pipeline-state.md`, and `audit.md` after acceptance. No AI service or complete B/X play is included in the initial shell.
